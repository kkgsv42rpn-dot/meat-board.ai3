BOARDS BY BARUCH creates premium kosher meat boards, charcuterie boards, individual trays and custom spreads designed around your event.

From intimate Shabbat and Yom Tov gatherings to birthdays, weddings, engagements, corporate events, gifts and large catering occasions, every board is assembled with attention to presentation, flavor and detail.

Choose your size, choose your cuts, add your sauces, pickles, olives and custom requests, and let us create the board around you.
